DOLPHIN TABLET FLOATER - v3   (all parts PETG)
================================================

PARTS (main 3MF, one plate each)
  Plate 1  Dolphin        dolphin_float      204 x 114 x 83 mm, belly on the bed
  Plate 2  Tube           tube               stands up, dolphin-thread end on the bed
  Plate 3  Sleeve + Cap   sleeve, cap_washer_pause

WHAT CHANGED IN v3
  Only the dolphin: straight, smooth swept-back dorsal fin. (v2 already had: one smooth body (no lumps) and a plain smooth base, no wave ripples.)
  Tube, sleeve and cap are the same as v1, so parts you already printed still fit.

TEST PACK (from v1)
  socket_test     - screw tube_test into it: checks the dolphin socket thread + collar seat
  tube_test       - short tube: one slot row, both threads, the collar with tick marks
  sleeve_test     - short sleeve: must turn freely on tube_test but not wobble
  cap_0.25 / cap_0.35 - two thread clearances. Keep the one that screws on smoothly.
                    cap_0.25 is the real cap, so if it fits you can use it on the floater.
  leak_test_puck  - print with the dolphin settings, leave it in the hot tub overnight.
                    If it still floats high and gives no bubbles when squeezed under water,
                    the dolphin settings are watertight.

SLICER SETTINGS (FlashStudio field names)
  Dolphin:  Wall loops 3, Top shell layers 5, Bottom shell layers 5,
            Sparse infill density 10%, Sparse infill pattern Gyroid
            Enable support ON, Type Tree (auto), On build plate only ON
            (needed under the beak, chin, flippers and tail flukes)
  Tube:     Wall loops 4, Sparse infill density 15%, no supports
  Sleeve:   Wall loops 3, no supports
  Cap:      Wall loops 4, Top shell layers 5, Bottom shell layers 5,
            Sparse infill density 25%, no supports

PAUSE FOR THE WASHERS (cap)
  The washer pocket is from 2.0 mm to 10.0 mm high (8 mm tall).
  In Preview, drag the layer slider to the FIRST layer above 10.0 mm,
  right-click the slider, and choose "Add pause".
  When it pauses, drop in 1/2" fender washers (38 mm outside), STAINLESS so they
  never rust: about 4 washers at 2 mm thick, or 5 at 1.6 mm. The top of the stack
  must be at or just under the top of the pocket. Then resume.

HOW IT WORKS
  - The tube screws into the dolphin belly until the collar seats.
  - The sleeve slides over the tube from the bottom, then the cap screws on
    and clamps the sleeve.
  - Adjust: loosen the cap a quarter turn, turn the sleeve, retighten the cap.
      pointer on the LONG tick   = fully open
      pointer on a MEDIUM tick   = closed
      SHORT ticks                = in between (about 2/3 and 1/4 open)
  - Load tablets: unscrew the cap, hold the dolphin upside down, drop in up to
    8 tablets (25.2 x 16 mm), screw the cap back on.
  - The blowhole on top of the head is the air vent, so the tube fills with
    water and every tablet gets wet.
  - Calculated float level: with 8 tablets and 4 washers, the water line sits
    about 3.5 to 4 cm up the dolphin (about 3 cm without washers). Its back, fin, head and tail stay dry.
